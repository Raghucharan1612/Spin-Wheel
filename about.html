Our story

SpinWheel started with a simple frustration: buying a used car was opaque, stressful and full of hidden surprises. We set out to fix that with a marketplace where every car is inspected across 200 points, every price is transparent, and every step — from test drive to RC transfer — is handled for you.

Today we serve buyers and sellers across 25+ cities with certified cars, doorstep test drives and instant finance. Our promise is unchanged: no haggling, no hidden charges, no compromises.
