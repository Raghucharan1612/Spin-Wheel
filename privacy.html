Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. Your trust matters — here's how we handle your data.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email, phone number and city when you create an account, list a car or contact us. We also collect usage data like pages viewed and saved cars to improve your experience.

2. How we use your information

Your information helps us provide and personalise our services: showing relevant cars, processing finance enquiries, sending price-drop alerts you opt into, and responding to support requests. We never sell your personal data.

3. Cookies & local storage

SpinWheel uses your browser's local storage to remember your theme, selected city, wishlist and session. These stay on your device and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

4. Data sharing

We share data only with trusted partners required to deliver a service you requested — for example, financing partners when you apply for a loan. All partners are bound by strict confidentiality obligations.

5. Security

We use bank-grade encryption and industry best practices to protect your information. While no system is perfectly secure, we continuously review and strengthen our safeguards.

6. Your rights

You can access, update or delete your account information at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@spinwheel.in.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach our privacy team via the Contact page.
